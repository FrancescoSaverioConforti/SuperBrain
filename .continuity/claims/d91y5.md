Implemented crates/codegraph-index/src/incremental/{mod,pipeline,indexer}.rs: first end-to-end wiring of RepositorySource->parse->extract->promote->lineage->edges->CodeGraph (pipeline.rs), plus CodeGraphIndexer (atomic publication mirroring B1's RealIndexer two-phase discipline: claim generation from an independent monotonic counter under a short lock, rebuild lock-free, compare-and-publish under a second short lock, strict > not >=). parser_for moved from edges::calls (pub(super)) to parse::mod (pub); calls.rs re-exports it.

Two real bugs found and fixed during implementation, independently re-confirmed by review (not just self-claimed): (1) generation claiming via max(published)+1 let two concurrent callers with nothing published yet claim the same generation — fixed with a separate always-incrementing counter; (2) SymbolSnapshotId hashes the owning commit, so an unchanged symbol re-promoted at a new commit gets a different id than before — feeding both old (historical, lineage-from endpoint) and new snapshots into resolve_calls together doubled/ambiguated resolved callers. Fixed by building the edge-resolution pool from current snapshots only, splicing historical lineage endpoints into the graph's final pool only after edge resolution runs.

Invalidation scope: re-parses only changed files actually present at the new commit; typed edges recomputed over the FULL current pool (not just changed snapshots) because resolvers match by name pool-wide — deliberate, proven by a dedicated under-invalidation test. Lineage propagated forward via lookup built from prior graph's own edges, survives later unrelated commits.
