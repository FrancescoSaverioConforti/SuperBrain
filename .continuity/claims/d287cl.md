End-to-end audit verification (commit 4045e6b = M5 Task 7) found all 12 reverified security invariants hold, but surfaced two real defects:

1. P0, confirmed build-breaking: crates/storage/src/audit_writer.rs (M5 Task 2) has no #[cfg(windows)] gate on its `mod` declaration or its `use windows::...`/LockFileEx calls, while the `windows` crate dependency itself is correctly declared only under [target.'cfg(windows)'.dependencies] in storage/Cargo.toml. storage (and therefore brain-api-audit-worker, mcp-server, both example binaries) cannot compile at all on non-Windows. Confirmed this breaks an otherwise-consistent #[cfg(unix)]/#[cfg(windows)] discipline already established pre-M5 in atomic.rs/handle_safety.rs/vault.rs -- audit_writer.rs is the one module that skipped it.

2. P1, confirmed: approve_cli.rs hardcodes NullAuditSink for run_cli_durable's best-effort sink layer, and reject has no durable-hooks path at all (by Task 7's own scope). Net effect: approving a proposal is durably audited end-to-end; rejecting one leaves zero audit trail anywhere beyond the proposal store's own status field. Needs an explicit user decision (accept as documented scope vs. extend a future task), not more investigation.

Recommendation given: do not close M5 until at least Defect 1 gets a narrowly-scoped fix. Full defect/invariant/crash-coverage matrices are in this session's transcript, not reproduced here.
