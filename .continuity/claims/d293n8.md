The audit log grows without bound; every spawn_worker call rescans the entire log from byte zero via recover_tail's startup chain-verification. No size cap, time-based rotation, archival, or multi-segment format exists anywhere in brain-api-audit-worker or storage. Not a correctness concern at current low-volume supervised usage; blocks sustained high-volume or long-running unattended production use.

Exit criterion: a designed retention/rotation policy, including how scan_chain/recover_tail would handle a multi-segment log.
Owner: unassigned -- must be explicitly picked up by a future milestone.
