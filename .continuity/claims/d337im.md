Rejected "document that caller must serialize" in favor of an actual OS-level lock owned by mcp-audit-bridge itself (std::fs::File::try_lock, same primitive storage::audit_writer already uses -- no new dependency). Must cover the entire critical section (registration resolve through final checkpoint/registration save), fail closed immediately on contention (no blocking wait), and release automatically on process death (no stale marker file). Implemented as crate::bridge_lock in mcp-audit-bridge; verified with a real separate-process kill test and real multi-thread tokio::spawn race test, not just in-process simulation.
