Caught and reproduced by final whole-branch review: committing note-a then propose_and_commit-ing NOTE-A with note-a's current version silently overwrites note-a's record, destroying the original note. This breaks the plan's own "version check scoped per note id" and "retry with fresh version" guarantees. The plan's original [a-zA-Z0-9_-]+ charset (mixed case) would still have this bug.
